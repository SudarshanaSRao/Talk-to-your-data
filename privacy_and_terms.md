# Privacy Policy & Terms of Use for Talk-to-Your-Data

_Last updated: 18th November, 2025_

## 1. Privacy Policy
Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights when using the Talk-to-Your-Data application (“the App”).

### 1.1. Data You Upload
- You may upload files (PDF, CSV, XLSX, DOCX, JSON, etc.) for analysis.
- Files are processed in memory only and are not stored, logged, or persisted.
- When your session ends or the app resets, all uploaded data disappears automatically.

### 1.2. Use of API Keys
- You have to provide your own API key for LLM providers (Anthropic Claude).
API keys are:
  - Stored only in your session memory.
  - Never saved, logged, or shared.
  - Sent only to the LLM provider you choose.

### 1.3. Data Sent to Third-Party LLM Providers
- The App may send your questions and extracted file content to the selected LLM provider.
- Third-party LLM providers may store activity logs according to their own policies.
- You are responsible for reviewing their Privacy Policies.

### 1.4. No Persistent Storage
The App does not store:
  - Uploaded files.
  - Chat history.
  - Personal data.
  - API keys.
- Logs containing user content.

### 1.5. Analytics and Logging
- Standard platform-level logs (Streamlit / hosting provider) may record technical metadata such as errors or request frequency.
- These logs do not contain your uploaded files or API keys.

### 1.6. Cookies and Tracking
- The App does not use analytics trackers or marketing cookies.
- Streamlit may use basic session cookies to maintain app state.

### 1.7. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age.

### 1.8. Your Rights
- Since the App does not store any personal data, access or deletion requests do not apply.
- You may remove all session data by simply closing or refreshing the app.

### 1.9. Changes to this Policy
- Updates may occur periodically.
- New versions take effect immediately once published.
